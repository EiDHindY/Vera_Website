Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who We Are

VERA Pet Care is a brand of Red Wolves Egypt. We manufacture and distribute pharmaceutical-grade pet care products in Egypt and the UAE. You can reach us via WhatsApp through the contact buttons on any page of this site.

2. Data We Collect

When you visit our site, the following data is collected automatically:

  • Analytics data (Google Analytics 4): Pages visited, session duration, device and browser type, country. This data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Meta Pixel data: Interaction tracking for advertising purposes on Meta platforms (Facebook & Instagram). This is governed by Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Preference data: Your selected market (Egypt or UAE) and language, stored locally in your browser via localStorage — never sent to our servers.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal data unless you initiate contact with us via WhatsApp.

3. How We Use Data

  • Analyze site performance and improve user experience.
  • Measure the effectiveness of digital advertising campaigns.
  • Understand which products attract the most interest from visitors.

We do not sell your data to any third party, nor use it for purposes not listed here.

4. Cookies

Our site uses two types of storage:

  • Essential (localStorage): Saves your market and language preferences locally in your browser. Required for the site to function correctly.
  • Analytical & Advertising cookies: From Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, used to analyze traffic and deliver relevant advertisements.

You can disable analytical cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting core site functionality.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share aggregated usage data with:

  • Google LLC — for analytics services. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — for advertising services. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify, Inc. — our hosting provider. Your IP address may be logged for security purposes.

6. Data Security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. The site is hosted on Netlify with full SSL encryption. All WhatsApp communications are end-to-end encrypted by WhatsApp.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you (which is near zero).
  • Request deletion of any data related to you.
  • Opt out of Analytics tracking via browser extensions such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.
  • Opt out of Meta personalized ads via your Meta account Ad Preferences.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. For material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us via WhatsApp using the button available on any page of this site.
