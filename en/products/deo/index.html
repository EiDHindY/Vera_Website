Active Ingredients

Four scientific complexes engineered for the harmony of fragrance and dermal health

A sophisticated cationic polymer matrix that binds to the hair shaft, smoothing the cuticle, eliminating static, and delivering a silky, show-quality finish after every application. Maintains moisture lock between baths, keeping the coat supple and easy to manage.

A curated blend of fine fragrance molecules specifically tested for use on pet skin — not a commercial perfume, but a precision-formulated aromatic complex that interacts with the coat's natural oils to create a lasting, distinctive scent profile without disrupting skin biology.

A synergistic hydration system combining Aloe Vera, Glycerin, and Panthenol that penetrates the coat to restore and seal moisture levels, preventing dryness and flakiness between grooming sessions. The Panthenol component repairs damaged hair shafts from within.

The entire formula is suspended in a precisely calibrated pH-neutral carrier that matches the biological acidity of pet skin. This protects the skin's acid mantle, making the mist completely safe for daily use without disrupting the natural microbiome or causing irritation over time.

Application Method

How to Use

01
Correct Distance

Spray from 20–30 cm from the coat. Avoid eyes, nose, and mouth.

02
Even Distribution

Move your hand continuously during spraying for an even coat across back, sides, and chest.

03
Brush to Finish

Once dry (approx. 1 minute), brush through the coat for maximum gloss and velvet texture.
