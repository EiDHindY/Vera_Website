Premium Care Ingredients

An exclusive fragrance blend designed specifically to last for days on your pet's coat. It provides a fresh and clean scent that eliminates natural pet odors without causing any respiratory irritation.

A signature scent that lasts for days after washing, ensuring your pet remains exceptionally fresh and clean.

An advanced blend of Polyquaternium-7 and Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride. This cationic system provides exceptional detangling, reduces static electricity, and leaves the coat silky smooth and easy to comb.

Advanced conditioning agents prevent hair knotting and leave it silky soft with a healthy shine.

A formula rich in natural Glycerin that acts as a moisture magnet, drawing hydration into the hair follicles and skin layers, preventing dryness and flaking caused by frequent bathing.

How to Use

Bathing Routine

01
Wet Thoroughly

Wet the coat completely with warm water.

02
Lather & Massage

Apply shampoo and massage gently into a rich lather.

03
Leave for 3-5 min

Leave the lather on for 3 to 5 minutes to let the conditioning agents work.

04
Rinse Well

Rinse thoroughly with water and dry.
