Professional Application

How to Use

01
Wet Thoroughly

Use lukewarm water to thoroughly wet the coat before applying shampoo.

02
Apply & Massage

Apply an appropriate amount of shampoo and distribute through the coat, massaging gently down to the skin.

03
Contact Time: 5–10 min

Leave on for 5–10 minutes to allow full therapeutic action — this is critical for efficacy.

04
Rinse Thoroughly

Rinse completely with clean water until all shampoo is removed from the coat.

✓Safe for dogs and cats aged 12 weeks and older
✗Not for use on animals under 12 weeks of age

✗Avoid direct contact with eyes and mucous membranes
✓Store away from direct sunlight at temperatures below 30°C
