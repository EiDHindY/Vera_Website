Pharmaceutical Formulation

Active Ingredients

Precision-calibrated at a clinical concentration, Salicylic Acid acts as a potent epidermal exfoliant that dissolves the protective waxy cuticle of arthropod parasites — directly compromising their dermal defenses. Simultaneously, it performs keratolytic action on the pet's skin, removing the dead cell layer that harbors parasite eggs and larvae, while maintaining a clean, receptive surface for the spray's therapeutic agents.

Our proprietary Neuro-Motor Complex (NMC) is a pharmaceutical-grade neuro-active compound precision-targeted at the biological Achilles' heel of ectoparasites. By binding to the parasite's acetylcholinesterase receptors, it triggers immediate, irreversible neural cascade failure — causing rapid, complete paralysis within minutes of contact, making escape impossible.

A pharmaceutical-grade broad-spectrum antimicrobial agent used in surgical procedures. At the VERA concentration, it delivers immediate bactericidal and fungicidal action on the bite wound and surrounding skin tissue, preventing the secondary bacterial infections and inflammatory responses that extend suffering well beyond the parasite itself.

A high-performance skin regenerator that triggers rapid healing of tissues damaged by bites and inflammation, restoring the skin's natural barrier. Allantoin promotes keratinocyte proliferation — accelerating the replacement of bite-damaged tissue with healthy new skin cells, dramatically reducing recovery time and preventing scar formation.

Intense botanical hydrator and soothing matrix for Flea Allergy Dermatitis (FAD) relief. It provides a protective layer that calms irritated skin instantly while its natural antioxidants protect healing tissue from oxidative damage caused by inflammatory responses.

Functions as a precision pH calibrator and gentle exfoliant that maintains the entire formula at the optimal acidity level for pet skin — ensuring the other active ingredients work at peak efficacy while respecting the natural acid mantle that forms the skin's primary immune defense.

Professional Application

How to Use

01
Shake Well

Shake the bottle thoroughly before each use to ensure active ingredients are evenly distributed.

02
Direct Application

Spray directly onto affected areas — coat, skin around ears, between toes, tail base — at 15–20 cm distance.

03
Massage In

Gently work the spray into the coat to ensure contact with the skin surface where parasites reside.

04
Leave to Dry

Allow to air-dry naturally. Do not rinse. The formula continues working as it dries.
